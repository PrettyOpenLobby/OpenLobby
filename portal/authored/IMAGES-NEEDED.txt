Images the authored pages reference but this repository does not ship
=====================================================================

The menu pages for content 0010 (DIRGE of CERBERUS) and 0011 (FANTASY EARTH)
draw their menu with three small pieces of chrome art that the development
tree borrowed from the FINAL FANTASY XI menu. That art is Square Enix's, so it
is not included here. Supply your own three files under each directory, or
copy the equivalents out of your own portal capture
(wh000.pol.com/pml/game/ff11/img/ holds the FFXI originals of the same names).

Both directories want the same three files:

  wh000.pol.com/pml/game/0010/img/mn01s.png
  wh000.pol.com/pml/game/0011/img/mn01s.png
      PNG, 225 x 24 pixels, 8-bit palette. The top cap of the menu column.
      The page draws it at 229 x 19.

  wh000.pol.com/pml/game/0010/img/mn02s.ang
  wh000.pol.com/pml/game/0011/img/mn02s.ang
      PlayOnline animated image (.ang container), 225 x 27 pixels per cell,
      6 cells. One menu row plate; the page shows cell 0 at rest and cell 1
      on hover. Drawn at 229 x 26, repeated once per menu entry.

  wh000.pol.com/pml/game/0010/img/mn03s.png
  wh000.pol.com/pml/game/0011/img/mn03s.png
      PNG, 225 x 30 pixels, 8-bit palette. The bottom cap of the menu
      column. The page draws it at 229 x 26.

Nothing else on these pages needs a server-side image. The title cards
(file:/systembg/dc.png, file:/systembg/fe.png) and the transparent click
plate (file:/img_s/general/im01s.png) are read from the client's own install.

The JongHoLow page (wh000.pol.com/pml/game/jan/index.pml) draws SE's own logo
and button plates from wh000.pol.com/pml/game/jan/img_s/, which is part of the
portal tree you supply; it needs nothing beyond that tree.

The 0008 (FINAL FANTASY XIV) and 0013 (EverQuest II) pages reference no
images at all.
