          Start is 0x20 - both measured live, as is the
          queue count. Changes land on the caller’s next poll, not
          instantly: the client asks on its own schedule.

GM chat - 

        
Delivered is not rendered. A line here really does
          reach the client - authserv relays it and logs it - but
          nothing we send has yet been seen to draw. A T takes
          its speaker from the client’s member table and no relayed speaker
          has ever been admitted to it (FACTS §“GM CHAT, THE ROOM
          ITSELF”). So the raw and nick boxes are the point, not a debug
          leftover: the encoders are written against the client’s parsers
          with no surviving capture to check them against, and this is where a
          wrong guess costs one line instead of a rebuild.

GM Call requests

        
Submitted from the Viewer's GM Call screen and
          filed by the gmd service, which answers the GM Call UDP
          band on 51112 and drops each decoded request into
          data/gm-calls/. Read-only: this is the ticket, and
          gmd answers the handshake, the queue count and the room
          redirect on its own. Nothing here replies - the chat itself rides
          the auth band (#gmchat001) and is still conducted by hand.

Announcements

        
One entry feeds every news surface the Viewer has: the
          login-screen ticker (latestnews.pml - by far the most-requested file on the server), the Information
          section’s per-category lists
          (news<N>.pml), a detail page per
          story that the ticker headline links straight to
          (<serial>.pml), and our own substitute
          Information page. Saving only writes the store; nothing reaches a client
          until you Publish.
