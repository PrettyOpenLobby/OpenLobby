Announcements

        
One entry feeds every news surface the Viewer has: the
          login-screen ticker (latestnews.pml - by far the most-requested file on the server), the Information
          section’s per-category lists
          (news<N>.pml), a detail page per
          story that the ticker headline links straight to
          (<serial>.pml), and our own substitute
          Information page. Saving only writes the store; nothing reaches a client
          until you Publish.

The password above is checked by the
        in-client account screens. The lobby login checks something
        else - the NICK token it recorded the first time this account signed
        in - and changing the password does not touch it. Tick the box only
        if they are locked out of the lobby: it makes the next client to connect
        as this account trusted on sight.

      

        
        
      

    

  


  
  

    

      
Login tokens for 

      
The lobby records the NICK token once per client and
        refuses that client for ever if it changes - which reads on the
        console as “incorrect PlayOnline ID or password” while
        the same account still works on the PC. Clear the row for the client that
        is locked out: its next sign-in is trusted on sight and records a fresh
        token. The other clients, the account token and the password are all
        untouched.
